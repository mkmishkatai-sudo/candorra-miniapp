How Candorra works

Advertisers pay for real ad views. A share of that real payment goes to you. Nothing is invented - every reward traces back to an advertiser's real payment.

Candorra may add a small top-up from its own daily budget so an ad pays more than its share. When that budget is used up, the reward goes back to the real share.

Withdrawals are reviewed by a person before they're sent. No fee, ever, to request or receive one.
