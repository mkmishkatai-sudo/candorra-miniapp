Why the rate can change

The launch rate is deliberately set at the conservative end of the only published figure we could verify for Telegram Mini Apps. Once real payments are measured, the rate is reset weekly to match them, and the current rate is shown here.

Withdrawals

Reviewed before sending, paid on TON in USDT, no fee to you. If a payment is delayed we say so. We never show "completed" before it is.

Fraud checks and appeals

Accounts are scored on several signals together, never one. We cannot identify your device, and we don't claim to. If your account is limited, you can appeal and a person reviews it.
