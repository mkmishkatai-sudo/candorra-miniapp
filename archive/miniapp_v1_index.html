Trust Center

The math behind every reward.

Where rewards come from

Every reward is funded by a real advertiser paying for a real action — a video watched, an offer completed. We never invent a balance.

How the split works

A share of what the advertiser actually pays is credited to you. The exact split is shown on every single reward, not just in a policy page.

Withdrawals

Every withdrawal is reviewed before it's sent. No fee is ever required to request or receive one.

Fraud & appeals

Accounts are risk-scored on real activity patterns, never on a single signal. If you're flagged, you can appeal — every review has a path back.
